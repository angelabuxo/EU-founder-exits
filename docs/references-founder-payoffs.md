# Benchmarks and literature for founder payoffs

Papers and reports that give the numbers this project compares against. These were collected earlier for a separate founder-economics project and checked for public URLs on 28 October 2026.

They are cited, not redistributed: only links appear here, no PDFs. Figures are as the sources report them, with page numbers where we have them; re-check a figure in the source before it goes in the paper.

## Founder payoffs: the US benchmark

- **Azevedo, Scheuer, Smetters & Yang**, *Dilution vs Risk Taking*. NBER Working Paper 34512 (Nov 2025): https://www.nber.org/papers/w34512. Revised version, July 2026: https://eduardomazevedo.github.io/papers/azevedo-scheuer-smetters-yang%202025.pdf
  - **July 2026 version:** 88% of founders exit with zero, and the top 1% capture 79% of value. The November 2025 version said 84% and the top 2% capture 80%.
  - Dollar figures are after a flat personal capital-gains tax of 28% (20% in the November version). Pre-tax mean payoff: $7.8M (July), $9.0M (November).
  - The only large founder-level payoff study found. It is American, and this project asks the European version.

## European VC exits and returns (company, deal or fund level, not founder level)

- **Axelson & Martinovic**, *European Venture Capital: Myths and Facts*. LSE FMG Discussion Paper 753 (2016): https://www.fmg.ac.uk/sites/default/files/publications/DP753.pdf
  - 35,798 VC-backed companies with a first round in 1980–2011.
  - Lifetime exit probability: IPO 6.2% in Europe against 13.1% in the US (Table 5A, p.24); trade sale 34.0% against 43.7%.
  - Exit hazard ratio for Europe about 0.77 with controls (Table 8, p.27). Nordic country coefficient −0.161, the closest to the US (Table 13, p.37).
  - First-round post-money median $4.53M against $9.65M (Table 3, p.22). Conditional on exit, returns are the same in Europe and the US.
- **Signore, Prencipe et al.**, *The European venture capital landscape: an EIF perspective, Vol. III: Liquidity events and returns of EIF-backed VC investments*. EIF Working Paper 2017/41: https://www.fi-compass.eu/sites/default/files/publications/eif_wp_41.pdf
  - 3,592 EIF-backed seed and start-up investments, 1996–2015, of which 2,065 exited.
  - Investor multiples: 57% below 0.25x, 78% at or below cost (Fig. 4, p.17). Weighted average 1.16x, median 0.12x (p.19). Pareto tail α ≈ 2.45 (p.26).
- **Le Pendeven, Manigart et al.**, "Practices of European and American venture capitalists: homogeneity and heterogeneity at work". *Small Business Economics* (2026), doi:10.1007/s11187-026-01211-w. Open postprint: https://orbilu.uni.lu/bitstream/10993/68462/1/%282026%2c%20SBE%29%20Le%20Pendeven%20et%20al.%20-%20Practices%20of%20European%20and%20American%20venture%20capitalists.pdf
  - VCs self-report about a third of investments failing in both the US (32%) and Continental Europe (34%) (p.8).
- **Vlerick Business School**, "45 per cent of European venture capital investments fail or do not secure high returns" (12 Apr 2023): https://www.vlerick.com/en/insights/45-per-cent-of-european-venture-capital-investments-fail-or-do-not-secure-high-returns/
  - Press summary of the survey behind Le Pendeven et al.
- **British Business Bank**, *UK Venture Capital Financial Returns 2025* (Dec 2025): https://www.british-business-bank.co.uk/sites/g/files/sovrnj166/files/2025-12/uk-vc-returns-2025-report.pdf. The 2024 edition: https://www.british-business-bank.co.uk/sites/g/files/sovrnj166/files/2024-11/uk-vc-financial-returns-report-2024.pdf?attachment
  - Fund-level pooled TVPI for 2002–2020 vintages: UK 1.84, rest of Europe 1.85, US 1.95 (2025 edition, p.4).
- **Atomico**, *State of European Tech 2025* (Nov 2025): https://soet-2025.cdn.prismic.io/soet-2025/aRzSCmGnmrmGp_9i_Atomico_Report2025.pdf
  - Europe accounts for 10% of global exits by value, down from 16% in 2024 (ch. 4).

## Nordic and Danish ecosystem

- **DI and Venture Insights**, *The Startup Barometer 2026: Growth and Capital in the Danish Startup Ecosystem* (Mar 2026): https://www.danskindustri.dk/globalassets/politik-og-analyser/analyser/2026/3/ivarksatterbarometret-2026/startup_barometer_2026_en_di.pdf
  - CVR-based, about 3,000 firms with a priced external round of DKK 1M or more, 2016–2025.
  - 94% still registered after 5 years, but acquired firms count as alive (p.10).
  - Acquisitions per year rose from 19 to 91, a lower bound with no deal values (p.27).
  - Founders typically hold through a holding company (p.7). The external count this project checks against.
- **PitchBook**, *2026 Nordic Private Capital Breakdown*: https://pitchbook.brightspotcdn.com/81/62/bdb2ffcd4add9d798a4b6509122a/2026-nordic-private-capital-breakdown.pdf
  - Nordic deal and exit counts and values for 2025.
- **Dealroom and HSBC Innovation Banking**, *Nordics report 2024* (data as of 5 Sep 2024): https://content.dealroom.co/uploaded/2024/09/Nordics-report-2024-Dealroom-x-HSBC-Innovation-Banking-final.pdf
  - Nordic VC totals. Cited only; Dealroom's platform terms forbid automated collection, but a published report can be cited.
- **Karlsen, Kisseleva, Mjøs & Robinson**, *Are Some Angels Better than Others?* NBER Working Paper 33231 (Dec 2024, revised July 2026): https://www.nber.org/papers/w33231
  - Norwegian register data on every private equity transaction, 2003–2018. Angel returns are profoundly right-skewed. Investor level, not founder level.
- **Ring**, *Taxing Entrepreneurial Wealth: Evidence from Norway, 2021–2025*. NBER Working Paper 35854 (Oct 2026): https://www.nber.org/papers/w35854
  - The marginal wealth tax on business equity rose from 0.47% to 0.88%. About 74–98 excess departures, concentrated in the top 0.1%.
  - No excess departures of startup founders, inventors or startup investors against Nordic controls. Unlisted shares are taxed on book equity.
- **Braunerhjelm, Broström & Åstebro**, *Does Academic Entrepreneurship Pay?* CESIS Working Paper 289 (2012): https://static.sys.kth.se/itm/wp/cesis/cesiswp289.pdf
  - Swedish registers on 478 academics who became full-time entrepreneurs: similar earnings, about three times the income risk.
- **Garnadt, Füner, Stahl & Tåg**, *Successful Entrepreneurs Come From the Top of the Earned Income Distribution*. CRC TR 224 Discussion Paper 693 (2025): https://www.wiwi.uni-bonn.de/bgsepapers/boncrc/CRCTR224_2025_693.pdf
  - Swedish register data; "success" means survival with 5+ employees.
- **Rigsrevisionen**, beretning 13/2025 on EIFO's green investments (Apr 2026): https://cdn.rigsrevisionen.dk/rigsrevisionen/Media/639128831444029093/sr1325.pdf
  - Background on the Danish state investor only.

## Central and Eastern Europe

- **Fazekas & Becsky-Nagy**, "Where is the EXIT? … Hungarian JEREMIE Investments". *Montenegrin Journal of Economics* 22(2), 45–56 (2026), doi:10.14254/1800-5845/2026.22-2.4: https://dea.lib.unideb.hu/bitstreams/0c29f0da-58a9-4e56-9a3e-e4faef51d244/download
  - All ~340 companies funded by Hungary's JEREMIE programme, 2010–2016.
  - Outcomes: 104–105 third-party sales, 73–76 management buy-backs, 91–95 failures; about 60 not exited by 2022. No exit values (Table 2, p.51).
- **Invest Europe**, Central and Eastern Europe report 2024 (released June 2025). Exact URL not confirmed; it is on Invest Europe's research pages.
- **Dealroom and Vestbee**, *CEE Report 2025*: https://content.dealroom.co/uploaded/2025/03/Dealroom-CEE-Report-2025.pdf
  - Answered 403 to scripts when checked; cite only.

## Founder backgrounds and policy

- **Endeavor Insight**, *Where Do Unicorns Come From? Studying the Career Journeys of the Top $1+ Billion Founders* (Sep 2023). The career paths of unicorn founders; a reference for founder-persona questions. Public URL not confirmed.
- **Campello & Junqueira**, *Tax Incentives and Venture Capital Risk-Taking: Evidence from the QSBS Program*. NBER Working Paper 35418 (Jul 2026): https://www.nber.org/papers/w35418
  - How the US QSBS exclusion changes VC risk-taking: more failures and more unicorns.
